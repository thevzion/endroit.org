# Landing exploration: direction contract

Status: local exploration on the exact `ecosystem-2026-08-02` revision.
Not accepted, not committed, not delivered. Human Content, Design and CTA
review has not happened.

## Editorial thesis

**A harness runs the agent. Endroit runs the work around it.**

One idea owns the page: the unit of work stops being a code change and becomes
a *dispatched consignment*: one written objective, an itemised manifest of
exactly which owned sources are aboard, a declared destination, and a
signature block that never leaves the human's hands. Everything else on the
page is that one idea seen from a different party's copy.

The page refuses the two arrangements this category always ships: the dark
developer-tool hero with a fake terminal and a feature grid, and the
"agent memory / better prompts" framing. Endroit is neither an agent runtime
nor a memory product, and the page says so in the same vocabulary it uses to
say what Endroit *is*.

## First proof

The **manifest**, in the first viewport, static, no JavaScript: `ABOARD` lines
with the reason each one is aboard, and `NOT ABOARD` lines struck through in
oxide red with a reason code. Exclusion is as legible as inclusion. It answers
"how does context reach an agent?" before the visitor has scrolled, and it is
the one mechanism no competitor's landing shows.

## Visual idea

**The Consignment Set**: a bound book of numbered multi-part forms.

It belongs to Endroit because a multi-part form is the only everyday artefact
that already separates the four things this product exists to keep separate:

- **shipper** (you: direction, judgment, consent), the white *original*;
- **carrier** (the harness: model, tools, sandbox), the *canary* copy;
- **consignee** (the Site: its own source, history, permissions), the *blue*
  copy;
- and the **manifest** itself, which is context resolution written down.

The stock colour of a sheet names whose authority that section is about. That
single rule carries the product's hardest claim in material rather than in a
diagram. The carbon set also literalises projection: one original you edit,
compiled copies for `AGENTS.md`, `CLAUDE.md` and Skills, so the copies are
output, never the source.

Type is semantic rather than decorative: **pre-printed** matter (what the
framework provides) is tracked grotesque caps in soft ink; **filled-in** matter
(your actual work) is monospace struck in full ink; **stamped** matter (state
and consent) is boxed oxide. No webfont is added; the identity lives in ruling,
tint blocks, field boxes, stamps and stock.

## Interaction grammar

Two enhanced objects, each explaining exactly one product behaviour; both
complete and honest with JavaScript off.

1. **The carbon set**: switch copies over one owned source to see the provider
   projections compiled from it. Without JavaScript every copy is printed in
   document order.
2. **The signature block**: `Retain` → `Close No. 0142` → `Open No. 0143`.
   Each stamp is a separate human act; the ledger line survives, the transcript
   does not. Without JavaScript the four states are printed in sequence with a
   note saying so.

Motion is the form's own: a stamp strike and a carbon impression. Nothing else
moves. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes both.

## Deliberately removed from the current landing

- The bench logic-analyzer world entirely: graticule screens, phosphor traces,
  status lamps, amber latch, oscilloscope framing.
- The nine `MODULE · X` plates, a catalogue rhythm that made every claim the
  same size.
- The three-intent resolver tab widget: three near-identical demonstrations
  collapsed into one manifest.
- The `CAL` tag on every module plus the `Alt+I` inspector, a second parallel
  information system. Its job is now done once, in the open, by the printed
  **Schedule A, claims and proofs** at the foot of the page.
- The stacked slogan sequence (eight punchlines) and the uppercase category
  kicker.
- The separate "legend" and "craft" modules, which restated claims already made.
